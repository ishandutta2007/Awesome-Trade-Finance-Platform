# Awesome-Trade-Finance-Platform

## Top Trade Finance Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Letters of Credit, Guarantees, Documentary Collections, Supply Chain Finance, Digital Trade Documents & Multi-Bank Collaboration*

**Last updated: August 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Trade Finance**. These systems digitize and manage traditional trade instruments (LCs, guarantees, collections), enable supply-chain finance, support electronic documents, and facilitate collaboration between banks, corporates, and logistics providers.



**Examples** include Surecomp, Finastra Trade Innovation, China Systems Eximbills, Komgo, Contour, Traydstream, TradeSun, Coriolis Technologies, GT Nexus, and Enigio (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Fully production-grade open-source trade finance platforms are rare. This section is expanded with the strongest available blockchain/DLT demos, Letter of Credit smart-contract projects, verifiable-credential experiments, and related open tools that banks, fintechs, and developers can study or adapt.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Surecomp (Rivo / DOKA)](https://www.surecomp.com/)**  

  Long-standing trade finance specialist offering digital platforms for guarantees, LCs, document exchange, and bank-corporate collaboration.



- **[Finastra Trade Innovation](https://www.finastra.com/)**  

  Enterprise trade and supply-chain finance platform used by global banks for end-to-end instrument processing and open-banking style connectivity.



- **[China Systems Eximbills](https://www.chinasystems.com/)**  

  Trade finance solution focused on documentary credits, collections, and related processing for banks.



- **[Komgo](https://www.komgo.io/)**  

  Network-oriented digital trade platform particularly strong in commodities, document authenticity, and multi-bank collaboration.



- **[Contour](https://www.contour.network/)**  

  Blockchain-based network for digital letters of credit, enabling faster, paperless LC processing among banks and corporates.



- **[Traydstream](https://www.traydstream.com/)**  

  AI-powered trade document processing and compliance platform that automates checking of LCs and related documents.



- **[TradeSun](https://www.tradesun.com/)**  

  Digital trade finance and compliance solutions focused on document digitization and risk reduction.



- **[Coriolis Technologies](https://www.coriolis.tech/)**  

  Trade finance and compliance technology provider supporting banks with digital processing and analytics.



- **[GT Nexus (Infor Nexus)](https://www.infor.com/)**  

  Supply-chain and trade visibility platform that supports financing and collaboration across global logistics networks.



- **[Enigio](https://www.enigio.com/)**  

  Digital original document technology (trace:original) used for creating and managing electronic trade documents with legal integrity.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Hyperledger Trade Finance & Logistics demos](https://github.com/HyperledgerHandsOn/trade-finance-logistics)**  

  Educational Hyperledger Fabric application demonstrating Letter of Credit and proof-of-shipment workflows among exporters, importers, and banks.



- **[Smart Bank LC Workflow](https://github.com/ac12644/Smart-Bank-LC-Workflow)**  

  Open-source smart-contract implementation of a modern Letter of Credit process using blockchain for reduced friction and fraud risk.



- **[Corda Letter of Credit CorDapp](https://github.com/clydedacruz/LetterOfCredit)**  

  Example CorDapp allowing nodes to engage in a full Letter of Credit scenario with buyer, seller, and banks.



- **[Solidity Letter of Credit projects](https://github.com/)**  

  Multiple open-source Solidity implementations of LC workflows, escrow, and document approval logic for digital trade finance.



- **[TradeTrust / verifiable credentials for LCs](https://github.com/)**  

  Experiments using verifiable credentials and decentralized identity for issuance, presentation, and verification of Letters of Credit.



- **[Hyperledger Fabric trade finance applications](https://github.com/)**  

  Additional community projects implementing request, issue, and accept LC flows on Fabric networks.



- **[RWA and on-chain lending primitives](https://github.com/)**  

  Open protocols exploring real-world asset and trade-related financing on public or permissioned ledgers.



- **[Document authenticity and e-bill of lading tools](https://github.com/)**  

  Open experiments around electronic original documents and transferable records relevant to trade.



- **[Trade data and compliance open libraries](https://github.com/)**  

  Supporting open-source components for parsing trade documents, checking rules, and integrating with external data sources.



- **[Multi-party workflow engines adapted for trade](https://github.com/)**  

  General open workflow and orchestration tools that teams customize for LC and guarantee approval processes.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Hyperledger Fabric, Corda, and other DLT frameworks used as foundations for private trade networks.

- Open e-invoicing and electronic document standards implementations.

- Supply-chain visibility open-source projects that can feed trade finance decisions.

- Rules engines for automated document checking and compliance.

- Academic and consortium reference implementations of digital trade corridors.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Prototype with a **Hyperledger Fabric** or **Corda** Letter of Credit demo, extend smart contracts for specific instrument types, add document storage and verification layers (including digital original concepts), and integrate with existing core banking or ERP systems via APIs. For production use, most institutions still rely on regulated commercial platforms or tightly controlled private networks; open-source components are best used for innovation, proof-of-concept, and internal tooling rather than as a complete regulated trade finance system of record.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Trade finance involves regulated instruments, legal title to documents, and significant financial risk. Open-source and blockchain demos are valuable for education and experimentation but are not substitutes for production systems that meet banking regulations, legal enforceability of electronic documents, and operational resilience requirements.

- Always involve legal, compliance, and trade operations experts before deploying any solution in a live trade environment.
